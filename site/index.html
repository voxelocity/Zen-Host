How it works

Three steps to a running world

  1. 01

    Create a server

    Name it, choose Vanilla, Paper, Fabric, Forge or NeoForge, and pick your Minecraft version. Zen Host fetches and installs everything for you.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Drop in mods, set the gamemode and difficulty, tweak the game rules and hand out operator status — all from the dashboard.

  3. 03

    Hit start

    Copy the connect address, send it to your friends, and watch the console light up as they join.

Pick your flavour

Five ways to play

Switch between server types at any time — Zen Host handles the installer, the loader and the folder layout.

Vanilla

Pure Minecraft

The official server, exactly as Mojang ships it. Nothing added, nothing to break.

Paper

Fast · plugins

A tuned fork with big performance wins and full Bukkit & Spigot plugin support.

Fabric

Lightweight mods

A slim, fast-updating loader. First to every new Minecraft version, and easy on RAM.

Forge

Classic modding

The veteran loader behind the biggest modpacks and the deepest mod libraries.

NeoForge

Modern fork

The community-driven successor to Forge, with faster releases and a cleaner API.
